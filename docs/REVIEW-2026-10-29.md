# Code and game-rules review — 2026-10-29 (v0.17.37, beta v0.111.0)

**Status: sections 1 and 2 implemented in v0.17.38** with Zaille75's decisions (2.1 status damage by creator, 2.2 relic
self-damage self-inflicted, 2.3 yes, 2.4 no, Ancients shown apart, 2.7 yes). 2.12–2.14 and 2.16 need no code; sections 3
and 4 are left as ideas.

Scope: the mod's capture layer (`Patches/`, `Capture.cs`), the stats engine (`Core/`), checked against the decompiled
game (`CreatureCmd`, `Hook`, `CardModel.OnPlayWrapper`, `CombatManager`, 269 powers, 300 relics, 122 monsters,
69 events, 66 potions, intents). Unit tests: 99/99 pass, but they only cover `Core/`; most of the misplay logic
(`BlockAlternatives.cs`, 968 lines) has no tests, and finding #1 is in that code.

Each finding has a file:line reference, the game code that proves it, how it shows in the stats, and a proposed fix.
Nothing was changed in the code. French names come from `lib/beta/names.txt`; monster names are the English class
names (they are not in that table).

Confidence: **Confirmed** = proved by the code (and the logs where possible). **Probable** = proved by the code, but
not seen in a log yet. **To discuss** = a rule choice for Zaille75.

## 1. Bugs

### 1.1 "Growing card never played" (Choix, kind `growth`) never triggers — Confirmed
- `BlockAlternatives.cs:612` adds the growing cards the player could afford to `GrowingAffordable` in
  `EndOfTurnChecks`. Right after that, `ResolveMissedChances` clears it at `:548-549`, along with `GrowingPlayed`.
  `ResolveCombat()` (end of the fight, `:377`) therefore always gets an empty set.
- Evidence: none of the logs in `logs/` has a single `MissedChance … growth` line.
- Cards affected: Algorithme génétique, La faux, Griffure, Mutilation, Saccage, The Ball.
- Fix: remove the two `Clear()` calls at `:548-549`. `Reset()` and `ResolveCombat()` already clear these sets.

### 1.2 Sacrifice counts Osty's HP as protection — Confirmed
- `Sacrifice.OnPlay` calls `CreatureCmd.Kill(Osty)`. `Kill` calls `LoseHpInternal(currentHp)` (`CreatureCmd.cs:380`).
  The mod's `LoseHp` postfix keeps that HP loss as pending. `KillCommand` sets the dealer to the acting player (the
  owner). Later it is reported as `DamageDealt(owner → pet, Hp = Osty's HP, Killed)`.
- In `StatsEngine.OnDamageToPlayerSide`, that hit takes the pet branch:
  - `AbsorbedByPet += Osty's HP` (shown as "Familier" protection);
  - each ally who gave Osty HP gets `PetAbsorbedForAllies` and `SavedPerAlly` (PV sauvés, Ange gardien);
  - `_petDied` is set, which then releases the pet's "saved HP" on the owner's next HP loss.
- Result: each Sacrifice adds 10–30 points of protection that never stopped any damage. Any other kill of one's own
  pet that is not damage would do the same.
- Fix: a hit on a pet whose dealer is the pet's owner, or that comes from `Kill`, should count as a pet death only,
  not as damage absorbed.

### 1.3 A player who dies during their own card stays "the acting player" for the rest of the fight — Confirmed (code)
- `CardModel.cs:1566-1590`: after `OnPlay`, `if (Owner.Creature.IsDead) return;` exits **before**
  `History.CardPlayFinished`. In that case the mod never pops `Capture.CardPlayers`, `Capture.CardPlays` or
  `BlockAlternatives.InProgress` (they are only cleared at the next `BeforeCombatStart`).
- In co-op the fight goes on, and `Capture.CurrentActingPlayer` returns the dead player every time no other card is
  being played (enemy turn, start of turn). From then on, the dead player is credited with:
  - **"Cartes données"** for every status card an enemy adds to an ally. Monsters call
    `AddToCombatAndPreview<…>(…, creator: null)`, and `CardGenerated` falls back to the acting player.
  - heals (`Healed`), energy (`HelpGiven energy`), cards drawn by relics, the giver of block that has a Card source,
    and the dealer of card-sourced kills.
- When it happens: a player killed by Thorns while attacking, by their own HP-cost card, by an Explosive-like effect…
- Fix: on `AfterDeath` of a player, remove that player's entries from the three stacks. A more robust option: pop in a
  postfix of `CombatManager.EndCardOrPotionEffect` (it is always reached, it sits in a `finally`). The potion side
  (`PotionUser`) has the same weakness if the user dies while drinking.
- Related risk, even without a death: `AfterCardGeneratedForCombat` with `creator == null` during **player A's** card
  play credits A with "Cartes données" when an enemy's reaction adds a status to player B. Only count it when the
  calling model is a player's card, power or relic (`CallingModelType()` already returns null when a monster is on
  the stack).

### 1.4 Cards played several times count as several choices — Confirmed (code)
- `CardModel.cs:1524-1556`: for Déflagration, Duplication, Forme résonnante, Droite-gauche, Amplification du signal and
  Relais, every extra play is a new `CardPlay`. All of them share the same `Resources` (energy, Stars paid once) and
  have `IsAutoPlay = false`; only `PlayIndex` differs.
- In `BlockAlternatives.OnPlayStarted` each extra play becomes its own `Play`, with the full `EnergySpent` and
  `StarsSpent`. Effects:
  - "Énergie pour rien", Larmes de Paël, `stars` and `starsWasted` are counted once per play (×2 or more);
  - weak attack, smaller block, Pen Nib and gâché are judged on each copy;
  - `CardsPlayed` and "Roi du combo" go up with the copies. The game's relics also count the copies, so this last
    point is a rule choice; the others are bugs.
- Fix: when `PlayIndex > 0`, attach the hits and the block to the first play's `Play` and leave the choice checks out.

### 1.5 Intangible and Dur à cuire on a player are never counted in "Évités" — Probable
- The spec (§C "Damage prevented — relics & powers") lists Intangible. But `IntangiblePower.ModifyDamageCap` (and
  `HardToKillPower`) cap the hit inside `Hook.ModifyDamage`, **before** block. When `ModifyHpLost` runs later, the
  amount is already ≤ 1, so it no longer changes and the mod's `HpLossModifiers` patch never sees it. The
  `ModifyDamage` postfix does not handle these caps either.
- Sources: Fantôme dans un bocal, card/relic effects that give Intangible, and any player-side Hard to Kill.
- Fix: in the `ModifyDamage` postfix, when a cap modifier belongs to the target player or pet, compute the damage
  without the cap (`ModifyDamageHookType.Additive | Multiplicative`). Credit the difference as a defense effect,
  resolved like Weak: only the part that would have gone past the block.

### 1.6 Strength down only counts when the enemy's total Strength is negative — Confirmed (code)
- `DebuffEffectPatches.cs` (StrengthPower case): `s.Owner == dealer && dealer.IsEnemy && s.Amount < 0`, credit
  `-s.Amount`.
- Example: an enemy with Rituel is at +3 Strength and the player applies −2. The hit is really 2 lower, but the mod
  counts **0**. At +1 total (−2 applied) it counts 1 instead of 2. This is common: Rituel cultists, and bosses that
  gain Strength every turn.
- Fix: credit the players' active Strength-down stacks (the engine already tracks them per applier in `_dots` /
  `_fifo` under `STRENGTH_POWER~DOWN`), capped at the hit's damage, × Weak / Vulnerable factors when present.

### 1.7 Heals after a won fight are counted nowhere — Confirmed (code)
- `CombatManager.cs:975` sets `IsInProgress = false`, then `:997` calls `Hook.AfterCombatVictory`. Sang ardent, Sang
  noir and Os à viande heal at that point. The mod's `Heal` prefix, outside combat, only handles Réparer. And
  `RunHistoryPatches.Read` skips every floor that had a fight (`!fight` filter).
- Result: the Soldat de fer's starting relic heals 6 after every fight, and none of it appears.
- Fix: outside combat, when the room is a `CombatRoom`, emit the heal as "Soins hors combat", with a new kind
  "afterFight" (or "relics").

### 1.8 Neow's heal counts as ~Max HP of "Soins hors combat" — Probable
- `AncientEventModel.BeforeEventStarted`: for **Neow** the game does `SetCurrentHpInternal(0)`, then
  `CreatureCmd.Heal(MaxHp)`. `Heal` adds `amountHealed = min(amount, MaxHp − 0) = MaxHp` to that floor's map history.
  The mod reads it as an event heal, so every player gets about +70/+80 "Soins hors combat (events)" at the start of
  each run.
- The other Ancients (Paël, Orobas, Tezcatara, Vakuu, Darv, Nonupeipe, Tanx…) heal to full (×0.8 with Weary
  Traveler) at the start of each act. Those heals are real, but automatic.
- To check in the next log. `RunHistoryRead` prints its dictionaries as type names, so the values are not readable:
  log them properly.
- Fix: skip the Neow floor. To discuss: show the Ancients' heal separately ("Ancien"), or leave it out.

### 1.9 Co-op: each player's incoming attack is computed for the local player — Confirmed (code)
- `AttackIntent.GetSingleDamage` ignores its `targets` argument. It computes the damage against
  `LocalContext.GetMe(...)`, the player sitting at that PC. `BlockAlternatives.IncomingDamage(creature)` relies on it
  for every player.
- Consequences: every "block needed / already covered" decision for **teammates** uses the local player's
  modifiers: Vulnérable, Tank (×1.5) / Abrité (×0.5), Intangible… This affects gâché vs inutile, unused energy,
  Énergie pour rien, smaller block, Larmes de Paël, Stars, wrong order (block) and the "why gâché" explanation.
  **Two installs compute different stats for the same player.** With Tank the gap is ×3.
- Monsters always target every player (`AttackCommand.FromMonster` → `TargetingAllOpponents`; no monster uses random
  targeting), so applying the intent to every player is right. Only the modifiers are wrong.
- Fix: compute `Hook.ModifyDamage(target = creature, dealer = enemy, intent.DamageCalc())` × `Repeats` ourselves,
  with `DebuffEffectPatches.Computing = true`. Today these intent computations also go through the `ModifyDamage`
  postfix as if they were real hits (they are only dropped because no `AfterModifyingDamageAmount` follows).

## 3. Stats not covered yet (ideas, for Zaille75 to pick)
- **Event outcomes** (already an open topic): cards gained / removed / transformed, relics, potions, and who chose in
  the 8 shared co-op events.
- **Cards stolen / recovered** (ThievingHopper), **enemy escapes**.
- **Afflictions on cards** put by enemies (Résonnant, Enfumé, Lié…): how many, and how many plays they blocked.
- **Enchantments** (Tezcatara's ember, Sharp…): the extra damage from an enchanted card is in Dégâts, but not shown as
  its own source.
- **Damage taken by kind**: attacks, Thorns / reactions, status cards, debuffs (Poison on the player).
- **Fight length** (turns, real time): in the roadmap "E".
- **Heals by source**: in-fight heals have no per-source detail (relic / card / potion).

## 4. Code-quality notes
- `CallingModelType()` walks the stack on every damage, block or kill without a card. It only works **before the
  first `await`** of the game command (documented). A future game patch that adds an `await` before the mod's prefix
  would silently make sources "Unknown". PatchCheck does not catch that: suggest a log counter of `Unknown` sources per
  fight.
- `BlockAlternatives` holds most of the misplay rules (energy, gâché, Stars, relics, order…) with no unit tests.
  Finding 1.1 would have been caught by a test. Proposal: move the decisions (`ResolveTurn`, `EndOfTurnChecks`,
  `UselessPlays`, relic triggers) to `Core/`, behind a small "turn snapshot" record the patch fills in.
- `ResolveMissedChances` clears the per-turn state in the middle of its resolution order: keep the clearing in one
  place (`ResolveTurn` end).
